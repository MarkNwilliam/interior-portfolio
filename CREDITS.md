## Dimension references

Layouts were checked against published anthropometric and interior-design guidance, including:

- Ernst Neufert, *Architects' Data*
- Julius Panero & Martin Zelnik, *Human Dimension and Interior Space*
- EN 12464-1 / CIBSE lighting guidance for residential task lighting

No stock photography is used on the site.
