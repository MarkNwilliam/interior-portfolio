## Dimension references

Layouts were checked against published anthropometric and interior-design guidance, including:

- Ernst Neufert, *Architects' Data*
- Julius Panero & Martin Zelnik, *Human Dimension and Interior Space*
- NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines
- EN 12464-1 / CIBSE lighting guidance for residential task lighting

### Formulas and rules applied

- **Percentile rule (Panero & Zelnik):** dimensions where a person *reaches* are sized to the 5th percentile (the smallest user); dimensions where a person or object must *clear* are sized to the 95th percentile (the largest user).
- **Seat height** = 5th-percentile popliteal height + 38 mm for shoes/clothing ≈ 430–450 mm.
- **Dining table top** 750–760 mm, leaving a ~300 mm thigh gap above a 450 mm seat.
- **Worktop** ≈ 900 mm (50–100 mm below the standing elbow); upper cabinets 450–600 mm above the worktop.
- **Sofa seat** 400–450 mm; **mattress top** 450–550 mm; **coffee table** ~400 mm.
- **Clearances:** ≥900 mm circulation; 750–900 mm dining pull-out (1100 mm to walk behind a diner); 380–460 mm sofa-to-coffee-table; 600 mm minimum beside a bed; ~600 mm per place setting; ≥1065 mm kitchen work aisle; work-triangle legs 1.2–2.7 m (total 4.0–7.9 m).
- **Reach zones:** primary reach 750–1500 mm above finished floor; eye/art line 1500–1600 mm; overhead storage ≤2100 mm.
- **Daylight:** window area ≥ 1/8 of the floor area; pendants 750 mm above the tabletop.
